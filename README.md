# Get Your Service — Pre-Order System

A campaign-based pre-order website for digital products — customers reserve a slot in advance, and delivery begins once the campaign goal is reached.

🔗 **Live Website:** https://pre-order.menifto.cyou/

## About the Project
Get Your Service — Pre-Order is a live pre-order platform built for taking pre-orders from customers for digital products. Instead of selling one-by-one, it runs campaigns: customers fill in a form to confirm their slot, and when the set number of orders for that campaign is reached, the campaign closes, the products are prepared, and delivery is sent directly to the customer's WhatsApp number.

The site is presented in Bangla for local customers, with a clear "how it works" flow, a section for running campaigns, and a section showing completed campaigns.

## Features
- Campaign-based pre-order system for digital products
- Pre-order form — customers fill it in to confirm their slot
- Goal / target system — campaign closes when the required number of orders is reached
- Product preparation starts after the campaign goal is fulfilled
- Delivery directly to the customer's WhatsApp number
- Running campaigns and completed campaigns sections
- Bangla-language, customer-friendly interface
- Deployed live on a custom subdomain

## How It Works
1. **Pre-order** — the customer fills in the form and confirms their slot.
2. **Goal fulfilled** — the campaign closes once the set number of orders is reached.
3. **Preparation** — the digital product is prepared for the customers.
4. **Delivery** — the product is delivered to the customer's WhatsApp number.
